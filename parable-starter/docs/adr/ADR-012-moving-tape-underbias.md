# ADR-012 — moving Preisach tape / underbiased record drive

Status: **selected for experimental SF012; independent review, founder listening
and hardware admission pending**. Issue55 authorizes this standalone selection;
it does not register a canonical processor or assign new astrology semantics.
[Gate0](../SF012_GATE0.md) records the baseline, audited sources, exact physical
assumptions, one selected fault, initial reference results and acceptance budgets.

Choose a positive Preisach population derived from Isaac Kramer's MIT relay
implementation, condensed into Gaussian-center coercivity-row boundaries. Use
fresh erased parcels moving through a finite Karlqvist record fringe, not one
stationary magnetization driven indefinitely. A parcel remembers its own field
history over~210us; source history and playback filters are transient audio
memory. Remanence after the parcel leaves forms the recorded spatial pattern.
No repeating tape loop, feedback, weak erase or borrowed SF006 ghost mechanism.
The declared finite fringe ends with an explicit return to exterior zero field.
An initial prototype omitted that projection and could report in-field rather
than remanent magnetization at extreme drive; reject its evidence for delivery.
Weak residual cycles outside the finite fringe are omitted, not measured absent.

The boundary `p=clamp(p,H-r,H+r)` tracks real switching/hold intervals. The
erf output integrates a continuous switching-center distribution; positivity
and `integral H dm=deltaPsi+r*total_variation(m)` establish passivity. Test
return-point/minor-loop/remanence, saturation, work and explicit-relay convergence.
This is a phenomenological scalar material, not measured domain-wall physics.

Use15ips,7um record/3um playback gaps,16um coating,25.5kA/m coercive scale,
145mT remanence and320nWb/m reference from published SM911 scales. Do not claim
a fitted SM911 deck: Gaussian distributions, single effective depth8.3um,
300turns/.6efficiency/.3um spacing, current gain and reduced equalization are
explicit assumptions. Ignore vector rotation, coupled stray fields, depth-
dependent recording, temperature/noise/granularity/core/amplifier limits; report
what those omissions leave unproven. Playback head averaging, induction and
integrating EQ remain explicit, not an arbitrary post-distortion tone label.

Change exactly the bias-only driver fundamental current12mA ->6mA peak at100kHz.
Material, audio current, heads, speed, filters and output gain remain identical.
Keep3mA severe candidate research only. Pre-code full-field studies predict
higher sensitivity/earlier compression and H3 at6mA; no fake gain trim, gate,
randomness or assured brighter/better sound. The source's general HF-bias trend
is not fully established by the effective-depth model; retain that limitation.

Compare native bias-phase-averaged event processing with an independently
implemented long-double resolved carrier and refinement. Use4x initially,
positive monotone prepared CDF interpolation, fixed row/event/phase capacities,
finite buffers, stopped configuration and no callback transcendental/allocating/
blocking work. Keep discarded resolutions/candidates and actual error/cost.
Host fidelity takes priority over unsupported embedded-readiness claims.

Reuse existing Soundforge PCM/statistics/excitation/guard/FFT/CLI/ARM/evidence
facilities and the **identical Centaur take03** source/preparation/rights, per
founder addendum. All three input groups share that phrase; raw/common-gain
and separately labeled RMS matching expose actual level changes. Controlled
magnetic/attack/chord/sweep tests do not replace the musical recording.

No Engine/preset/pool/firmware/living/phone/correspondence changes, goldens
refresh or other PR modification. Standalone16KiB state/24KiB text ceilings
are not available hardware budgets. Actual M7 object/call/layout/stack proof,
host cost, regressions/sanitizers and preserved firmware precede review; M7
cycles/latency/physical tape/codec and founder preference remain separate gates.
Next after listening: independently evaluate the accuracy/cost of any proposed
embedded approximation against this retained physical reference and WAVs.
